# Indian-Food-Choices-Their-Impact-and-Significance-Dashboard


-> This dashboard is an interactive tool designed to analyze Indian food choices across demographics and age groups. It provides valuable insights through visually engaging and intuitive charts, graphs, and slicers.

-> Visualizations in the Dashboard

-> Page 1: Demographic Overview
 This page provides a high-level breakdown of the dataset, focusing on audience demographics.

1. Bar Charts: Represent gender distribution and regional segmentation of respondents.
2. Pie Charts: Display the percentage distribution of key demographics like age groups, regions, or any specific categories.
3. Slicers: Enable filtering by gender, region, and other demographic attributes to explore targeted insights.

-> Page 2: Food Preferences Analysis
This page dives deep into the trends and preferences in food choices.

1. Stacked Bar Charts: Show popular food items or categories across different regions or demographics.
2. Clustered Column Charts: Compare food preferences by specific attributes like type, region, or occasion.
3. Pie Charts: Highlight the distribution of food preferences within key categories, such as vegetarian vs. non-vegetarian or regional cuisine breakdown.
4. Slicers: Allow dynamic filtering by food categories, regions, and preferences for granular analysis.

-> Page 3: Age Group Insights
This page analyzes food choices and their impact based on age groups.

1. Stacked Bar Charts: Showcase food preferences by age groups, highlighting generational trends.
2. Line Charts: Provide insights into the evolution of preferences over different age ranges.
3. Treemaps: Visualize the significance of food choices within each age group, emphasizing major contributors.
4. Donut Charts: Represent the percentage contribution of each age group to specific food categories.
5. Slicers: Enable filtering by age groups to focus on specific generational insights.

-> Slicers and Filters

1. Age Groups: Segment data by age ranges to uncover generational preferences.
2. Gender: Compare trends across male and female respondents.
Region: Analyze food choices by geographic location for regional insights.
